## 👤 About the Author & Community Vision

Hi, I am **Samuel Serra**, a Cybersecurity Professional dedicated to protecting enterprise infrastructures and cloud ecosystems. 

This repository is the cornerstone of my latest practical laboratory deployment, designed to mirror production-grade environments while mapping directly to the official objectives of the **Microsoft SC-300 (Identity and Access Administrator)** and **MD-102 (Endpoint Administrator)** certifications.

### 🎯 The "Why" Behind This Project
In modern cybersecurity, **Identity is the new security perimeter**. A vast majority of successful cyberattacks originate from compromised credentials, lateral movement, lack of strict permission segmentation, or poor asset standardization. 

As corporate cloud infrastructures expand exponentially, security teams require a deep, technical understanding of cloud-native and hybrid environments. This lab provides a dynamic, hands-on framework to show how proper Identity Governance and Endpoint Management can drastically reduce the corporate attack surface, isolate threats, and prevent critical operational incidents.
